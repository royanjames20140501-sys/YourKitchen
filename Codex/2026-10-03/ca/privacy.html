Your information, in plain language

Current app behavior · Last updated October 3, 2026

What stays on your device

Your language choice, saved recipes, recipe history, Food Lab ideas, taste profile, kitchen chat, meal plan, shopping list, game score, and recent searches (up to 20, kept for 30 days) are stored in this browser on this device. When you sign in with a configured account, personal kitchen data is also synced to your private account record. Clearing this browser’s site data removes its local copy. If the feedback or community storage service is not configured or cannot be reached, feedback and recipe submissions are kept in this browser on this device.

Feedback and community recipes

When the site’s storage service is connected, submitted site and dish feedback is stored in YourKitchen’s database (up to the latest 10,000 entries). Community recipes are visible to other visitors and the latest 500 are kept; up to 100 ratings per recipe and up to 1,000 reports are retained. Ratings appear publicly, while reports are sent privately to the site owner for review. The app does not include a name or IP address in feedback, rating, or report records. A temporary one-way hash of the visitor’s IP is used for rate limiting and expires after one hour for recipe submissions or one minute for feedback, ratings, and reports. Recipe submissions are shared as written, so do not include private information.

When you use AI

If you ask for AI recipe ideas or use the kitchen companion, your question and selected language are sent to the YourKitchen server and OpenAI to generate a response. The app keeps your recent chat history on this device and syncs it to your private account record when signed in. OpenAI’s API data controls say abuse-monitoring logs may retain some request content for up to 30 days by default, with exceptions; see OpenAI’s data controls. AI features need an internet connection.

Accounts

Email and password sign-in use Supabase Auth when the site owner adds a Supabase project URL and publishable key and runs the included database migration. Personal recipe data is stored in a private account record protected by row-level security. If those project settings are missing, the account form will not create an account and data stays on the device.

Analytics and advertising

This version does not include an analytics or advertising tracker.

Questions or changes

YourKitchen is a work in progress. The account service and feedback/community database must be configured by the site owner before sign-in, cross-device sync, public recipe sharing, ratings, or reports are available.
